Privacy

What the discussion system records

Public participation uses an alias. Email and technical security metadata are not shown to other readers.

Public data

Your chosen alias, comments, replies and timestamps may be public. Your email is private.

Private technical metadata

When you sign in or participate we may record IP address, browser, operating system, device class, referrer, language, time zone and screen size. Where the server can determine it, approximate country, region or city derived from IP may also be recorded.

We do not request GPS or precise device location.

Purpose

Technical metadata is used for security, abuse prevention, spam control, impersonation, threats, moderation and audit. It is private and limited to authorized administration.

Data minimization

The system should retain only technical data needed for security and moderation and review its retention periodically. Personal data is not sold and IP addresses are not published.
