Editorial method

What La Trama Pública aims to distinguish

01

Visible sources

Important factual claims should be traceable to a source.

02

Levels of certainty

Verified facts, testimony and interpretation should not be presented as the same thing.

03

Traceable corrections

Relevant changes should record what changed, when and why.

04

Editorial separation

Facts, documents, context and opinion are identified separately.
